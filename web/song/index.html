Song

Paste a chord sheet, send its chords to FoMni-1. Tap a saved song to send it and open its lyrics: the chord you're on lights up as you play, and tapping a chord there jumps the FM-1 to it. They go on the black keys in the order they come (your own chords come back when you clear it). As you play, the black key of the next chord blinks (with ENV / LFO when it needs the minor or the 7th). Tap ENV + LFO together to step back one chord; hold them 1.5 s to go back to the top. Chrome (Android or computer) or Edge, with the FM-1 on USB.
